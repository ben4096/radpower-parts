# Radpower parts

Sharing models and instructions for discontinued parts.

> **Unofficial community project.** Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rad Power Bikes. "Radpower" and "Rad Power Bikes" are trademarks of their respective owner and are used here only to indicate compatibility. All models are original work, measured and drawn from scratch; no manufacturer drawings or files are included.
